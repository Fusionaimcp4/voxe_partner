This Early Access SAFE pool is the first part of a larger planned raise for Voxe. Early supporters can participate now; the proceeds help cover legal, compliance, platform, and marketing costs as we prepare for the full round.

Planned raise: This invite-only pool lets you invest before the broader funding round opens. Terms and structure for that larger round — including the funding portal or platform we use — will be shared with investors once finalized.

What is a SAFE?

We are using a SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity), based on industry-standard Y Combinator templates. A SAFE is:

  • An agreement where you invest money now,
  • In exchange for the right to receive equity later, when the company does a priced equity round or another defined trigger event.

Key characteristics:

  • It is not a loan.
  • It has no interest rate and no fixed maturity date.
  • The number of shares you ultimately receive is determined by the SAFE’s terms (such as valuation cap and/or discount) at the time of conversion.

Friends & Family Pool: $50K = 10% Equity

For this invite-only round, we are setting aside a fixed pool of 10% of the company’s equity for a total of $50,000 in SAFE investments.

Economically, this means:

  • The entire $50,000 corresponds to 10% of the company.
  • Each investor’s share of that 10% is pro-rata based on their contribution to the $50,000 pool.

Example: If you contribute 10% of the total pool (i.e., $5,000 out of $50,000), your SAFE will be structured to represent approximately 1% of the company at the time of conversion (10% of the 10% pool).

The final legal SAFE documents govern the exact conversion mechanics, but the intent is that the Friends & Family pool collectively owns approximately 10% of the company once the SAFE converts.

Founder & Team Vesting

To align incentives with long-term company success, founder and key team equity will follow standard startup vesting:

  • 4-year vesting schedule,
  • With a 1-year cliff, and
  • Monthly vesting after the first year.

This means founders and team members earn their equity over time, as they continue to build the company. It helps protect investors by reducing scenarios where someone can leave early while still owning a large, unearned stake.

Risk Disclosure

Investing in early-stage startups is inherently risky. You should only invest if you can afford to lose 100% of your investment. There is no guarantee of liquidity, dividends, or a future exit.

This page is invite-only and shared for informational purposes. It is not a general solicitation or public offering. Any investment will be made only through formal legal documents (SAFE) that you should review carefully, and you are encouraged to consult your own legal and financial advisors.
