How It Works

From website URL to live support desk

Five steps, start to finish — no integration project, no months-long rollout.

01

Provide your URL

Businesses submit their website; no manual setup required to begin.

02

Knowledge extraction

Voxe crawls the site and ingests uploaded documents into a searchable knowledge base via RAG.

03

AI agent deployed

A chat widget, knowledge-backed chatbot, and helpdesk inbox go live, tuned to the business's own content.

04

Connect live data & actions

Commerce, CRM, and calendar integrations let the AI look up — and on connected e-commerce platforms, directly update — real order, inventory, and account data, once the customer is verified.

05

Human escalation

Routine, verifiable actions complete without a human. Requests that need judgment — disputes, policy exceptions, anything ambiguous — hand off to a live agent with full context attached.
