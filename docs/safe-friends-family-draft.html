SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity)

Friends & Family Early Access — Voxe

DRAFT — SUBJECT TO LEGAL REVIEW. This document describes the approved business intent for the Friends & Family Early Access SAFE. It is not a legally approved definitive agreement. It is not a standard Y Combinator SAFE. Y Combinator did not create or approve these economics. Blockchain Technology International LLC, operating as Voxe Desk, is currently a limited liability company; the precise form of equity or equity-related interest issued on conversion is subject to legal review.

This SAFE (this “Agreement”) is made as of (the “Effective Date”) between:

[Investor Name], an individual (the “Investor”), and

Blockchain Technology International LLC, a Texas limited liability company operating as Voxe Desk (“Voxe Desk” or the “Company”).

The Company and the Investor agree as follows:

1. Purchase Amount; Offering Limits

1.1 Purchase Amount.

The Investor is investing US $[Investment Amount] (the “Purchase Amount”) in the Company on the terms set out in this Agreement. The Purchase Amount is the actual amount of money contributed by the Investor.

The Purchase Amount under this offering must be at least US $1,000 and not more than US $5,000 per investor.

1.2 Aggregate Offering Maximum.

The Company will accept up to an aggregate of US $50,000 in Purchase Amounts under this Friends & Family Early Access SAFE offering (the “Aggregate Offering Maximum”).

1.3 No Current Valuation; No Fixed Ownership at Investment.

This offering does not establish a current valuation of the Company. This Agreement does not promise, and the Investor is not purchasing, a stated ownership percentage of the Company at the time of investment.

2. Early Supporter Conversion Amount

2.1 Multiplier.

This offering uses a uniform Early Supporter Multiplier of five (5×).

2.2 Conversion Amount.

The “Early Supporter Conversion Amount” equals the Purchase Amount multiplied by five (5).

Examples (for illustration of the calculation only):

  • US $1,000 Purchase Amount → US $5,000 Early Supporter Conversion Amount
  • US $2,000 → US $10,000
  • US $3,000 → US $15,000
  • US $4,000 → US $20,000
  • US $5,000 → US $25,000

The Early Supporter Conversion Amount is used solely for the future equity conversion calculation under this Agreement. It is not an amount paid, invested, or contributed by the Investor beyond the Purchase Amount.

3. Nature of this SAFE

3.1 Not Debt.

This SAFE is not a loan or other debt instrument. It does not accrue interest. It does not create an automatic repayment obligation merely because time passes.

3.2 Future Equity.

In exchange for the Purchase Amount, the Investor receives the right to be issued equity (or another equity-related interest) in the Company in the future upon the occurrence of certain events, primarily a Qualified Equity Financing (defined below), subject to the terms of this Agreement and the Aggregate Program Ceiling.

For legal review: The Company is an LLC. The definitive agreement should specify the precise form of interest issued on conversion (for example, membership interests or other equity securities) consistent with the Company’s then-current legal and capitalization structure.

3.3 No Present Ownership.

Until this SAFE converts pursuant to Section 4, the Investor does not have voting, distribution, or other ownership rights of an equity holder of the Company, except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

3.4 Custom Instrument.

This Agreement is a custom Early Access SAFE. It is not a standard Y Combinator SAFE. It does not include a valuation cap, discount, or MFN as terms of this offering.

4. Conversion upon Qualified Equity Financing

4.1 Qualified Equity Financing Defined.

A “Qualified Equity Financing” means a bona fide future financing in which the Company issues equity securities for cash to one or more investors and establishes an applicable company valuation or equity price for that financing.

Ordinary founder issuances, compensatory equity, Growth Partner Program awards, internal reorganizations, or conversions of existing instruments do not, by themselves, automatically constitute a Qualified Equity Financing solely for purposes of establishing the financing valuation under this Agreement.

For legal review: Precise qualification thresholds (if any), excluded transactions, and the definition of “applicable company valuation or equity price” (including capitalization conventions) should be finalized in counsel-approved definitive language.

4.2 Conversion Mechanics — Business Intent.

Upon the first Qualified Equity Financing after the Effective Date, this SAFE is intended to convert into equity of the Company using the Early Supporter Conversion Amount and the applicable company valuation or equity price established in that financing, subject to the Aggregate Program Ceiling in Section 5 and the definitive agreement.

Illustrative calculation only (not a promise of ownership):

  • Purchase Amount: US $5,000
  • Early Supporter Conversion Amount: US $25,000
  • If the applicable company valuation established in the Qualified Equity Financing were US $2,500,000:
  • US $25,000 / US $2,500,000 ≈ 1.00%

Actual conversion and resulting ownership depend on the definitive agreement, capitalization definitions, applicable financing terms, and the Aggregate Program Ceiling.

4.3 Documentation.

The Investor agrees to execute documents reasonably required to effectuate conversion in connection with a Qualified Equity Financing, provided the Investor is treated in a manner not materially less favorable, in the aggregate, than other holders of this Friends & Family Early Access SAFE program, taking into account investment size and the terms of this Agreement.

5. Aggregate 10% Program Ceiling

5.1 Ceiling.

The Friends & Family Early Access SAFE program has a maximum aggregate conversion ceiling of ten percent (10%) (the “Aggregate Program Ceiling”).

The Aggregate Program Ceiling is not an amount of equity sold today. It is not a guarantee that Friends & Family investors will receive 10% of the Company. It is the maximum aggregate ownership that this program may produce under its conversion mechanics, subject to the definitive agreement.

5.2 Application of the Ceiling.

The 5× Early Supporter Conversion Amount calculation applies normally until the Aggregate Program Ceiling becomes relevant. If the aggregate calculated ownership for all participating SAFEs under this offering would exceed the Aggregate Program Ceiling, the available program ownership is allocated among participating SAFE holders pro rata according to their Purchase Amounts.

Illustrative allocation if the ceiling is triggered and the Aggregate Offering Maximum is fully subscribed: an Investor with a US $5,000 Purchase Amount represents US $5,000 / US $50,000 = 10% of aggregate Purchase Amounts and would receive 10% of the available program allocation under that simplified illustration (approximately 1% of the Company). This illustration is not guaranteed ownership outside the definitive conversion mechanics.

6. Liquidity Event Before Conversion

6.1 Intended Treatment.

If a qualifying acquisition or other Liquidity Event (as defined in the definitive agreement) occurs before conversion under Section 4, the intended business treatment is that the Investor receives the greater of:

  • (a) the Investor’s actual Purchase Amount; or
  • (b) the amount the Investor would be entitled to receive under the agreed as-converted Early Supporter calculation,

subject to the definitive agreement, applicable law, and the Aggregate Program Ceiling.

The 5× Early Supporter Multiplier does not create a guaranteed 5× cash return.

For legal review: Define “Liquidity Event,” as-converted calculation mechanics for an LLC sale or similar transaction, payment priority, and form of consideration in definitive counsel-approved terms.

7. Dissolution

7.1 Intended Treatment.

If the Company dissolves before conversion, the intended treatment is return of the Investor’s actual Purchase Amount, subject to available assets, creditor claims, senior obligations, applicable law, and the definitive agreement.

The 5× Early Supporter Multiplier does not create a right to receive five times the Purchase Amount upon dissolution.

8. Termination

This SAFE is intended to remain outstanding until the earliest to occur of:

  • (a) Conversion under Section 4;
  • (b) Payment (or setting aside for payment) under Section 6 or Section 7; or
  • (c) A written agreement between the Company and the Investor to terminate this SAFE.

There is no automatic maturity or automatic repayment solely because time passes. Termination does not affect rights or obligations that have already accrued.

9.1 Organization and Authority.

The Company is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of its jurisdiction of formation, and has the power to execute, deliver, and perform its obligations under this Agreement.

9.2 Authorization.

All action required to authorize the execution and delivery of this Agreement and the performance of the Company’s obligations hereunder has been duly taken. 9.3 No Conflict.
