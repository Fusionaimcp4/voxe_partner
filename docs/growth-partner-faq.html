Growth Partner FAQ

Program: Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program (“GPP”)

Company: Blockchain Technology International LLC, operating as Voxe (“Voxe” or the “Company”)

Version: Draft 1.0 — October 2026

Framework & Terms Definitions & Attribution Milestone & Equity Schedule FAQ Program Overview

1. What is the Voxe Growth Partner Program?

The Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program (“GPP”) is a selective performance-based program for individuals who can make a meaningful and measurable contribution to the growth of Voxe.

Growth Partners may become eligible for equity awards by creating measurable, attributable, and durable growth in Voxe.

The standard GPP milestones are based primarily on:

  1. Qualifying Active Paying Customers (“QAPCs”); and
  2. Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”).

Both requirements must be satisfied for a milestone to be reached.

2. Is the Growth Partner Program a job?

No.

Participation in GPP does not, by itself, create an employment relationship with Voxe.

Unless separately agreed in writing, participation does not provide a salary, wages, employee benefits, guaranteed working hours, an executive title, or employment rights.

A Growth Partner may separately enter into an employment, contractor, advisory, executive, affiliate, investment, or other relationship with Voxe.

Those relationships would be separately documented.

3. Do I need to invest money in Voxe to become a Growth Partner?

No.

GPP is based on measurable contribution to Voxe's growth rather than an investment of capital.

Any opportunity to invest in Voxe, including an Early Access SAFE or another investment opportunity, is separate from GPP and subject to its own terms and documentation.

A person may potentially be both an investor and a Growth Partner, but one relationship does not automatically create rights under the other.

4. Is GPP the same as the Voxe Affiliate Program?

No.

The programs are separate.

The Affiliate Program generally provides cash commissions for qualifying customer referrals or acquisitions according to the applicable Affiliate Program terms.

GPP provides the opportunity to become eligible for equity awards after satisfying defined customer and recurring-revenue milestones.

Participation in the Affiliate Program does not automatically make someone a Growth Partner.

5. Can I participate in both the Affiliate Program and GPP?

Yes, where approved under the applicable programs.

A Growth Partner may separately participate in the Voxe Affiliate Program.

Where permitted, the same qualifying customer may:

  • generate affiliate compensation under the Affiliate Program; and
  • contribute toward GPP customer and recurring-revenue milestones.

The two forms of compensation remain separate.

Affiliate compensation is governed by the then-applicable Affiliate Program terms.

6. What affiliate commission will I receive?

GPP does not establish or guarantee an affiliate commission rate.

Affiliate commission rates, payout periods, attribution rules, providers, and other affiliate terms are governed separately by the Voxe Affiliate Program.

This allows Voxe to modify its Affiliate Program or change affiliate technology providers without changing the fundamental GPP structure.

7. How do I become a Growth Partner?

Participation in GPP is selective.

A person may become a Growth Partner through direct invitation or acceptance by Voxe.

Someone may also begin as a Voxe affiliate, demonstrate an ability to acquire and retain customers, and later be invited into GPP.

Submitting an Expression of Interest, referring a customer, participating in the Affiliate Program, or discussing GPP with Voxe does not automatically establish Growth Partner status.

8. What if I already referred customers before becoming a Growth Partner?

Customers acquired before your GPP Effective Date do not automatically count toward GPP milestones.

When admitting an existing affiliate or contributor into GPP, Voxe may review the person's existing qualifying customer portfolio and expressly recognize some or all eligible customers.

If Voxe recognizes existing customers, the initial recognized QAPC count should be documented when the person enters GPP.

9. What is a Qualifying Active Paying Customer?

A Qualifying Active Paying Customer (“QAPC”) is generally a customer who:

  • is legitimately attributable to the Growth Partner;
  • has an active paid Voxe subscription;
  • completed the applicable qualification period;
  • remains active on the applicable Measurement Date;
  • is in good financial standing;
  • is not subject to a disqualifying refund, chargeback, or payment dispute;
  • is not fraudulent, duplicated, artificial, internal, or a test account; and
  • satisfies the other applicable GPP requirements.

A signup or lead alone is not a QAPC.

10. Do free trials count?

No.

Free trials, free accounts, leads, prospects, demo requests, mailing-list subscribers, website visitors, and similar activity do not count as QAPCs.

A trial customer may later become a QAPC after converting to a paid subscription and completing the applicable qualification period.

11. How long must a customer remain paying before the customer qualifies?

For a monthly subscription, the customer generally must complete:

Three successful consecutive monthly subscription payments.

For an annual subscription, the customer generally must remain an active paid subscriber for at least:

90 days.

All other QAPC requirements must also be satisfied.

12. Why doesn't a customer count immediately after subscribing?

GPP is intended to reward durable business growth rather than temporary signups.

The qualification period helps distinguish customers who establish a genuine paid relationship with Voxe from customers who subscribe briefly and immediately cancel, refund, or charge back their payment.

13. What happens if one of my customers cancels before I reach a milestone?

If the customer is no longer an active qualifying paying customer on the applicable Measurement Date, the customer does not count toward the QAPC requirement for that measurement.

GPP measures the qualifying active customer portfolio rather than lifetime historical customer acquisition.

14. What happens if customers cancel after my milestone has already been verified?

Normal customer churn after a milestone has been formally verified does not, by itself, reverse that verified milestone.

The active-customer and ARR requirements determine whether the milestone was earned at the applicable Measurement Date.

Once formally verified and documented under the applicable definitive agreement, the resulting award is governed by that agreement and its vesting provisions.

Fraud, manipulated attribution, fabricated customers, material misrepresentation, or similar misconduct may be treated differently under the definitive agreement.

15. What if a canceled customer comes back?

A customer who fully cancels and later returns to Voxe generally must complete a new Qualification Period before returning to QAPC status.

The reactivated customer does not become an additional customer merely because the customer subscribed again.

Once requalified, the customer continues to represent one QAPC.

16. What is Attributable ARR?

Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”) is the annualized recurring subscription revenue associated with QAPCs attributed to the Growth Partner.

For a monthly subscription:

Current Monthly Recurring Subscription × 12

For an annual subscription:

Current Annual Recurring Subscription

Attributable ARR generally excludes taxes, refunds, credits, chargebacks, and non-recurring revenue.

17. Does my affiliate commission reduce my Attributable ARR?

No.

Affiliate compensation and GPP Attributable ARR are separate calculations.

For example, if qualifying customers generate $100,000 of Attributable ARR, the GPP calculation remains $100,000 even if the Growth Partner separately receives affiliate commissions associated with those customers.

18. What happens when a customer upgrades?

The customer generally remains one QAPC.

The customer's Attributable ARR increases to reflect the current qualifying recurring subscription amount.

An ordinary paid-plan upgrade does not normally require the customer to repeat the original Qualification Period.

19. What happens when a customer downgrades?

The customer may remain one QAPC as long as the customer remains active, paying, and otherwise qualifying.

Attributable ARR decreases to reflect the customer's current qualifying recurring subscription amount.

An ordinary paid-plan downgrade does not normally require the customer to repeat the Qualification Period.

20. How are discounted customers counted?

Attributable ARR generally reflects the customer's actual recurring subscription obligation rather than an undiscounted list price.

The purpose is to measure the recurring revenue actually represented by the qualifying customer portfolio.

21. How does Voxe determine whether a customer belongs to me?

Voxe may use an Approved Attribution System and other reliable evidence.

Evidence may include:

  • affiliate or referral tracking;
  • referral links or codes;
  • CRM records;
  • documented introductions;
  • campaign records;
  • customer records;
  • billing records;
  • customer confirmation; or
  • other reliable evidence accepted by Voxe.

No third-party affiliate platform independently determines equity ownership.

22. What happens if two Growth Partners claim the same customer?

A customer ordinarily has one GPP attribution owner.

If multiple Growth Partners claim the same customer, Voxe may review the available evidence to determine the appropriate attribution.

Relevant information may include who originated the relationship, documented introductions, referral records, campaign attribution, timing, material contribution to conversion, and other available records.

The standard GPP does not use fractional customer splitting by default.

23. What are the standard GPP milestones?

The current standard milestone structure is:

MilestoneQAPCsMinimum Attributable ARRCumulative Equity Eligibility
Growth I100$100,000Up to 0.50%
Growth II250$250,000Up to 1.00%
Growth III500$500,000Up to 2.00%
Growth IV1,000$1,000,000Up to 3.00%

Both the QAPC requirement and Attributable ARR requirement must be satisfied.

24. What if I have enough customers but not enough ARR?

The milestone has not yet been reached.

For example:

100 QAPCs + $80,000 ARR

does not satisfy Growth I.

Growth I requires at least:

100 QAPCs AND $100,000 Attributable ARR.

25. What if I have enough ARR but not enough customers?

The milestone has not yet been reached.

For example:

80 QAPCs + $120,000 ARR

does not satisfy Growth I.

Both requirements must exist simultaneously using the same qualifying customer portfolio at the applicable Measurement Date.

26. Are the milestone percentages added together?

No.

The milestone percentages are cumulative.

The standard progression is:

Growth I → up to 0.50% total

Growth II → up to 1.00% total

Growth III → up to 2.00% total

Growth IV → up to 3.00% total

A Growth Partner reaching Growth IV does not receive 0.50% + 1.00% + 2.00% + 3.00%.

The standard cumulative GPP ceiling is up to 3.00%.

27. What happens when I reach a milestone?

Reaching the numerical threshold does not automatically issue equity.

The normal process is:

Performance → Milestone → Verification → Definitive Agreement → Equity Award → Vesting

When records indicate that a milestone may have been reached, the milestone is subject to Voxe verification.

Voxe may review customer attribution, payments, active subscription status, qualification periods, recurring revenue, refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, duplicates, fraud, and other applicable requirements.

28. Does the dashboard or affiliate system give me equity automatically?

No.

A dashboard, affiliate report, referral counter, website display, email, or automated calculation does not itself issue equity.

These systems may provide information used during milestone verification.

Actual equity rights are governed by the applicable definitive agreement.

29. When do I actually receive an equity award?

After Voxe formally verifies a milestone, the Growth Partner becomes eligible for the applicable award under GPP, subject to execution of the applicable definitive agreement.

The definitive agreement establishes the legal form of the award and the terms governing it.

GPP participation alone does not issue equity.

30. How does vesting work?

Under the standard GPP framework, each incremental milestone award generally vests:

Monthly over 12 months, with no additional cliff

beginning on the effective date specified in the applicable definitive agreement.

Each newly earned incremental milestone award may have its own 12-month vesting schedule.

31. Can I work toward the next milestone while my previous award is vesting?

Yes.

A Growth Partner does not need to wait for one milestone award to finish vesting before working toward the next milestone.

For example:

Growth I is verified and the applicable 0.50% award begins vesting.

The Growth Partner may continue growing the qualifying customer portfolio.

If Growth II is subsequently verified, the additional award associated with Growth II may begin its own 12-month vesting schedule.

The Growth I vesting schedule does not restart.

32. What does "incremental award" mean?

Because milestone percentages are cumulative, later milestones generally create an award equal to the difference between the newly verified cumulative level and the previously verified level.

Under the standard schedule:

MilestoneIncremental EligibilityCumulative Eligibility
Growth IUp to 0.50%Up to 0.50%
Growth IIUp to additional 0.50%Up to 1.00%
Growth IIIUp to additional 1.00%Up to 2.00%
Growth IVUp to additional 1.00%Up to 3.00%

Each incremental award may have its own vesting schedule.

33. Do I have to maintain the exact milestone customer count during vesting?

Not merely to prevent normal customer churn from reversing an already verified milestone.

Once a milestone has been formally verified, ordinary subsequent customer churn does not automatically erase it.

Vesting is instead governed by the applicable definitive agreement and the Growth Partner's continued good standing.

A future milestone still requires the Growth Partner to satisfy that future milestone's QAPC and ARR requirements at a new Measurement Date.

34. What does "good standing" mean during vesting?

Good standing generally means that the Growth Partner has not:

  • voluntarily withdrawn where continued participation is required;
  • been terminated for fraud, material misconduct, or material breach;
  • materially manipulated customer or attribution information;
  • materially violated applicable GPP requirements; or
  • triggered another forfeiture provision contained in the definitive agreement.

The definitive agreement controls the exact vesting and forfeiture rules applicable to an award.

35. Is 3% the maximum available under GPP?

Under the standard GPP milestone framework, yes.

The standard cumulative ceiling is:

Up to 3.00%

associated with the Growth IV milestone of at least:

1,000 QAPCs + $1,000,000 Attributable ARR

Contribution beyond Growth IV may lead to discussion of a separate strategic, executive, employment, advisory, distribution, or other relationship.

Additional equity is not automatically created by exceeding Growth IV.

36. Does "up to 3%" mean I automatically own 3% of Voxe after reaching Growth IV?

No.

Growth IV establishes eligibility for the applicable cumulative award under GPP after verification and subject to the definitive agreement.

The exact legal form, ownership mechanics, vesting, capitalization treatment, and other rights associated with an equity award are determined by the applicable definitive agreement.

The GPP milestone itself is not an equity issuance.

37. What happens if Voxe later raises investment capital?

Future financing, equity issuances, restructuring, conversion events, or other capitalization changes may affect ownership percentages.

The treatment of any GPP equity award in those circumstances depends on the actual legal instrument and definitive agreement governing that award.

GPP does not guarantee that a stated percentage will remain economically unchanged following future company capitalization events.

38. What type of equity will I receive?

The exact legal form of a GPP equity award is established in the applicable definitive agreement.

Depending on Voxe's legal and capitalization structure, different forms of equity or equity-related interests may be appropriate.

The GPP Framework itself does not promise a particular legal instrument.

39. Can I sell my equity immediately?

Not necessarily.

Equity in a private company may be subject to vesting, transfer restrictions, securities-law requirements, company agreements, contractual restrictions, and the absence of any public market.

The applicable definitive agreement determines the specific rights and restrictions associated with an award.

40. Is the equity guaranteed to become valuable?

No.

Participation in GPP does not guarantee that Voxe will increase in value, raise additional financing, be acquired, conduct a public offering, make distributions, generate profits, or provide liquidity for an equity interest.

An equity interest in a private company may ultimately have little or no economic value.

41. What happens if Voxe changes GPP in the future?

Voxe may modify, replace, suspend, or discontinue GPP prospectively as the company evolves.

However, a prospective program change is not intended to retroactively eliminate a milestone award that has already been formally verified and documented under an executed definitive agreement, except as permitted by that agreement, including circumstances involving fraud, material misrepresentation, breach, or similar misconduct.

42. Can Voxe change the affiliate provider without changing GPP?

Yes.

GPP does not depend on a particular affiliate technology provider.

Voxe may use different referral, affiliate, CRM, billing, or attribution systems over time.

Historical GPP attribution is based on Voxe's applicable records and program rules rather than ownership of data by a particular affiliate platform.

43. Can I represent myself as an employee or official representative of Voxe?

Not solely because you participate in GPP.

Participation does not automatically authorize a Growth Partner to enter contracts for Voxe, change pricing, make guarantees, promise product functionality, bind the company, or represent themselves as an officer, employee, or authorized legal representative of Voxe.

Any such authority must be separately granted by Voxe.

44. What growth methods can I use?

Growth Partners may generally use lawful and appropriate methods such as:

  • direct business development;
  • professional relationships;
  • referrals;
  • partnerships;
  • demonstrations;
  • content;
  • social media;
  • communities;
  • search and content marketing;
  • industry networks;
  • events;
  • outbound sales; and
  • other legitimate customer-acquisition methods.

Growth Partners may not use fraudulent, deceptive, unlawful, or materially misleading practices.

45. What happens if someone manipulates the program?

Fabricated customers, artificial account splitting, fraudulent subscriptions, attribution manipulation, circular payments, false information, or similar conduct may result in customer disqualification, milestone disqualification, suspension, termination, or remedies available under the applicable definitive agreement.

GPP is intended to reward genuine business growth.

46. Does one company with many users count as many customers?

Generally, no.

A normal customer organization with one Voxe subscription relationship ordinarily counts as:

1 QAPC

Adding users, seats, agents, integrations, departments, or features does not automatically create additional QAPCs.

Voxe may review genuinely independent subscriptions involving separate organizations, brands, locations, subsidiaries, or other commercial arrangements according to the applicable program rules.

47. Can I count my own company as a referral?

Not automatically.

A Growth Partner self-referral requires explicit Voxe approval before being counted as a QAPC.

This rule helps prevent artificial customer creation solely for the purpose of satisfying GPP milestones.

48. Who makes the final milestone determination?

Voxe administers and verifies GPP milestones using reasonably available business records.

No single affiliate platform, payment processor, CRM, dashboard, or other third-party system independently determines GPP equity eligibility.

Voxe may review relevant underlying records before formally verifying a milestone.

49. Are there tax consequences if I receive equity?

There may be.

Equity awards, affiliate compensation, and other benefits may have tax consequences depending on the participant, award structure, jurisdiction, and circumstances.

Growth Partners are responsible for obtaining their own tax, financial, and legal advice.

Applicable tax reporting, withholding, elections, or related requirements will be addressed according to the definitive award structure.

50. Which document controls if the FAQ is different from my agreement?

This FAQ is intended to explain GPP in plain language.

The Growth Partner Program Framework & Terms and Definitions & Attribution Rules provide the more detailed program rules.

For a specific equity award, the applicable executed definitive agreement controls where it conflicts with general GPP materials.

51. What is the main principle behind GPP?

The program is designed around a simple idea:

Voxe Growth Partners earn the opportunity for ownership by creating measurable, attributable, and durable growth in the Company.

GPP is intended to align meaningful company ownership opportunities with meaningful long-term value creation.
