The Solution

Voxe

An AI platform for automated customer operations. Provide your website URL and Voxe instantly deploys a fully operational support system powered by your knowledge and connected to live business data.

  • AI customer support powered by website knowledge and custom knowledge bases
  • Integrated helpdesk for human escalation and conversation management

Deployment takes minutes instead of weeks.

What Makes Voxe Different

Most AI chatbots can only answer questions from static knowledge bases. Voxe connects AI directly to business systems — and, once a customer is verified, acts on their behalf.

Operational AI in Action

Customer asks: “Where is my order?”
Voxe retrieves real-time order status from the company’s commerce system.

Customer asks: “What did I purchase last time?”
Voxe accesses customer purchase history through CRM or sales data.

Customer asks: “Is this product in stock?”
Voxe checks live inventory data before responding.

Customer says: “My address autofilled wrong, can you fix it before it ships?”
Voxe verifies the order, confirms it hasn’t shipped, and updates the address directly — no ticket, no wait.

Customer says: “Can you just cancel that order? Found it cheaper elsewhere.”
Voxe confirms the order is unpaid and cancels it on the spot.

Result: Voxe becomes an AI-powered operational layer that verifies, decides, and acts—not just a chatbot answering FAQs. Anything requiring judgment — disputes, policy exceptions, paid-order changes — still escalates to a human with full context attached.
