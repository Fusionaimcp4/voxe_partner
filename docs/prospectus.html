01 — Executive Summary

What Voxe Is

Voxe is an AI-first customer support platform that converts a company's website and documents into a live AI + human helpdesk system. Businesses provide their website URL, and Voxe automatically builds an AI knowledge base, deploys a support chatbot, and creates a helpdesk inbox — connected to the business systems that matter, including e-commerce platforms, CRMs, and calendars.

Voxe's AI agents go beyond answering questions about orders, products, accounts, and support requests — once a customer is verified, they can securely execute the request itself, escalating anything that needs human judgment (see Section 3 for how). The platform is built for small and growing businesses that need enterprise-grade support without enterprise complexity, per-seat fees, or per-resolution pricing.

02 — The Problem

Customer support infrastructure is broken for most businesses

Current solutions such as Zendesk, Intercom, and Salesforce Service Cloud were built for enterprises with dedicated implementation teams. For everyone else, they create more problems than they solve:

  • Complexity — Setup requires weeks of configuration, integration work, and technical knowledge.
  • Cost — Most platforms charge per agent seat or per resolution, so cost climbs as a business grows — exactly when margin matters most.
  • Limited automation — Most AI chatbots can describe a policy or a product, but the moment a request requires actually doing something — editing an order, cancelling a purchase, processing a refund — they hand the customer to a human. A support bot that can't act on a request is a slightly better FAQ page, not a support tool.
  • Fragmented infrastructure — Businesses stitch together a chatbot, a helpdesk, a CRM, automation tools, and a scheduler. Every seam is operational friction and extra cost.

Most small and mid-sized businesses simply cannot afford, or cannot operate, enterprise support infrastructure.

03 — The Voxe Solution

One AI-powered support system, deployed from a URL

Voxe unifies automation, human support, and operational data access into a single platform, automatically generated from a company's existing content.

Core Capabilities

Instant AI support deployment

Voxe extracts knowledge from a company's website, generates a chatbot, and builds a helpdesk system — in minutes instead of weeks.

Hybrid AI + human support

AI agents handle common inquiries automatically; complex issues are routed to human agents through the integrated helpdesk, with full conversation context attached.

Real-time business data access

Through API integrations, Voxe's AI agents can check order status, inventory, and purchase history — so it answers "where is my order?" correctly, not generically.

Multi-channel by default

Web chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and phone are powered by the same knowledge base, so answers are consistent everywhere.

Built-in helpdesk

A fully integrated helpdesk lets teams manage conversations, assign agents, and monitor customer interactions from one inbox.

Automated scheduling

Customers can book meetings directly inside a conversation, through integrated calendar scheduling.

Secure Action Execution

The gap between an AI that talks about a task and one that completes it is identity verification. Before Voxe touches an order, it matches an order number to a second identifier — the account email or billing name — against the live store record. That check is enforced in the workflow logic itself, not a prompt instruction the AI could be argued past. If the identifiers don't match, Voxe won't confirm order details at all, and offers to route the conversation to a human instead.

Shipping & billing address edits Order cancellations Policy-matched refunds Return requests Shipment & delivery tracking Identity verification

Write access is deliberately scoped to actions that are reversible, low-risk, or that clearly match a store's own stated policy — exact scope varies by integration. Anything that falls outside those conditions — a disputed charge, a partial refund outside policy terms, a change to paid order contents — is escalated to a human agent with the full order and conversation context already attached, rather than approved automatically.

From website URL to live support desk

Deployment is designed to remove the integration project that competing platforms require.

Provide your URL

Business submits its website — no manual content setup to begin.

02

Knowledge extraction

The site and any uploaded documents (PDF, DOCX, text) are chunked, embedded, and indexed for semantic search.

03

AI agent deployed

A chat widget, knowledge-backed chatbot, and helpdesk inbox go live, tuned to the business's own content and tone.

04

Connect live data & actions

Commerce, CRM, and calendar integrations let the AI look up — and on connected e-commerce platforms, directly update — real order, inventory, and account data, once the customer is verified.

05

Human escalation

Routine, verifiable actions complete without a human. Conversations that need judgment — disputes, policy exceptions, anything ambiguous — hand off to a live agent in the helpdesk, with full context attached.

07 — Product & Current State

A working, continuously developed platform

Voxe is in active production development, not a prototype. The platform is built on a modern, modular architecture:

  • AI Agent Layer — Handles customer conversations and reasoning, routed through a multi-LLM tool-calling layer that selects the right tool for each task.
  • Knowledge Engine — Processes website content and uploaded documents into a searchable knowledge base using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
  • Integration Layer — Connects the AI agent to business systems through APIs and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
  • Workflow Engine — Automates actions triggered by conversations, including booking and escalation.
  • Helpdesk Platform — Manages human support interactions, assignment, and monitoring.

Live Integrations

Shopify WooCommerce HubSpot Salesforce Pipedrive Google Calendar Custom systems via MCP

For the e-commerce integrations, catalog data — products, pricing, stock — syncs into the knowledge base on a schedule, while order, fulfillment, and refund status are queried live at the moment a customer asks, because that data changes by the hour. The same connection carries the write actions: address edits, cancellations, and policy-matched refunds execute against the live order record, not a cached copy.

Built-In Operational Maturity

Beyond the AI product itself, Voxe runs on production-grade infrastructure: authenticated accounts with two-factor authentication, Stripe-based subscription billing with usage metering, automated error monitoring, and a containerized deployment pipeline. This is the kind of foundation that supports paying customers today and scales with growth, not a demo environment.
