Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program

Draft — Subject to Definitive Agreement · Draft 1.0 — October 2026

Program Overview

The Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program is a selective performance-based program intended for individuals who can make a meaningful and measurable contribution to the growth of Voxe.

The GPP is built around a simple principle:

Growth Partners who create measurable, attributable, and durable growth in Voxe may become eligible to earn ownership in the Company.

Participation in the GPP is by invitation or acceptance by Voxe.

Submission of an Expression of Interest, participation in the Voxe Affiliate Program, referral of customers, discussions with Voxe, or receipt of GPP materials does not automatically make an individual a Growth Partner.

How the Program Works

GPP, the Voxe Affiliate Program, and any Voxe investment opportunity are separate programs with separate purposes, terms, and rights.

The program does not award equity based merely on time spent, marketing activity, leads generated, introductions made, promises of future contribution, social-media activity, or participation in the program.

Instead, equity eligibility is based primarily on two measurable business outcomes:

  1. Qualifying Active Paying Customers (“QAPCs”) attributable to the Growth Partner; and
  2. Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”) generated by those customers.

Both requirements must be satisfied for a Growth Partner to reach an applicable milestone.

For purposes of GPP, customer milestones are based on Qualifying Active Paying Customers (“QAPCs”), not total historical signups or lifetime customer acquisitions.

In addition to the applicable QAPC requirement, each GPP milestone requires a minimum amount of Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”).

A milestone is not satisfied unless both the applicable QAPC requirement and Attributable ARR requirement have been satisfied and verified.

Participation in GPP does not itself grant ownership in Voxe.

Affiliate Program (Separate)

GPP, the Voxe Affiliate Program, and any Voxe investment opportunity are separate programs with separate purposes, terms, and rights.

Contribution: attributable customer referrals or acquisitions.

Benefit: cash or other commissions according to the applicable Affiliate Program terms.

Affiliate compensation does not constitute equity.

Participation in the Affiliate Program does not automatically create GPP eligibility.

A Growth Partner may separately participate in the Affiliate Program. Where a person participates in both programs, affiliate compensation and GPP milestone calculations remain separate.

The commission rate, payout rules, tracking provider, qualification requirements, and other Affiliate Program terms are governed exclusively by the then-applicable Affiliate Program terms and may change independently of GPP.
