# Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program
## Definitions & Attribution Rules

**Draft — Subject to Definitive Agreement**

**Program:** Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program (“GPP”)  
**Company:** Blockchain Technology International LLC, operating as Voxe (“Voxe” or the “Company”)  
**Version:** Draft 1.0 — October 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

These Definitions & Attribution Rules establish how Voxe determines:

- which customers count toward GPP milestones;
- which recurring revenue counts toward GPP milestones;
- which Growth Partner receives attribution for a customer;
- when a customer becomes qualifying;
- when a customer stops qualifying;
- how upgrades, downgrades, cancellations and reactivations are treated; and
- how milestone measurements are performed and verified.

These rules supplement the Voxe Growth Partner Program Framework & Terms.

Where an executed definitive agreement applicable to a particular Growth Partner or equity award conflicts with these rules, the definitive agreement controls.

---

# 2. Key Definitions

## 2.1 Growth Partner

A **Growth Partner** is an individual or approved entity formally accepted by Voxe into GPP.

Receiving GPP information, referring customers, participating in the Affiliate Program, submitting an Expression of Interest, or discussing participation with Voxe does not by itself establish Growth Partner status.

---

## 2.2 GPP Effective Date

The **GPP Effective Date** is the date on which Voxe recognizes the participant as an active Growth Partner under GPP.

This date should be recorded for each Growth Partner.

Unless Voxe expressly approves otherwise in writing, customer acquisition occurring before the GPP Effective Date does not automatically count toward GPP milestones.

---

## 2.3 Qualifying Active Paying Customer

A **Qualifying Active Paying Customer (“QAPC”)** is a customer attributable to a Growth Partner who:

1. has an active paid Voxe subscription;
2. has completed the applicable qualification period;
3. is active on the applicable Measurement Date;
4. is in good financial standing;
5. has not received a disqualifying refund or chargeback;
6. is not fraudulent, duplicated, artificial, internal or a test account; and
7. satisfies the other applicable GPP requirements.

A customer is counted as **one QAPC** regardless of the number of users, seats, agents, integrations, features, or other resources associated with that customer account, unless Voxe expressly defines a different counting method for a particular commercial arrangement.

This prevents one business from being artificially divided into multiple customers merely to increase milestone counts.

---

## 2.4 Qualification Period

The **Qualification Period** is the minimum period a paying customer must successfully complete before becoming eligible to count as a QAPC.

For monthly subscriptions:

> **Three successful consecutive monthly subscription payments.**

For annual subscriptions:

> **At least 90 days as an active paid subscriber without a disqualifying refund, chargeback, cancellation, or other disqualifying event.**

Completing the Qualification Period does not override any other QAPC requirement.

---

## 2.5 Attributable ARR

**Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”)** is the annualized recurring subscription revenue associated with QAPCs attributed to the Growth Partner.

For monthly subscriptions:

> Current recurring monthly subscription amount × 12

For annual subscriptions:

> Current recurring annual subscription amount

Attributable ARR is based on the customer's **current qualifying recurring subscription value on the Measurement Date**, rather than the customer's original subscription value.

---

## 2.6 Measurement Date

The **Measurement Date** is the date or defined measurement period used by Voxe to determine whether a Growth Partner has satisfied a milestone.

The QAPC count and Attributable ARR are evaluated using the qualifying customer population applicable to that Measurement Date.

---

## 2.7 Approved Attribution System

An **Approved Attribution System** means a referral, affiliate, CRM, billing, campaign, customer-record, documented-introduction, or other tracking or attribution system designated or accepted by Voxe.

Voxe may replace or modify an Approved Attribution System without changing the fundamental GPP rules.

No third-party affiliate platform independently determines equity ownership or milestone entitlement.

---

# 3. What Counts as One Customer

The intent is to measure genuine independent paying customer relationships.

A normal customer organization with one Voxe subscription counts as:

**1 QAPC**

Adding employees, agents, seats, departments, channels, integrations, knowledge bases, or other functionality to that subscription does not create additional QAPCs.

If one legitimate business maintains multiple genuinely independent Voxe subscriptions for separate business units, brands, locations, or operations, Voxe may determine whether those subscriptions represent separate QAPCs based on their commercial independence.

Artificial subdivision solely or primarily intended to increase GPP customer counts is prohibited.

---

# 4. Monthly Subscription Qualification

A monthly subscriber does not count immediately when the first payment is made.

The customer must successfully complete:

**Payment 1 → Payment 2 → Payment 3**

and remain active and otherwise qualifying.

Once the third consecutive monthly payment has successfully completed, the customer may become a QAPC.

### Example

Customer subscribes January 10.

Successful payments:

January 10  
February 10  
March 10

Assuming all other requirements are satisfied, the customer may become a QAPC following the successful March payment.

If the February payment fails and the account becomes delinquent, the customer does not qualify merely because the original January payment succeeded.

---

# 5. Annual Subscription Qualification

An annual subscriber does not need to make three separate payments.

Instead, the customer must remain an active paid subscriber for at least:

**90 days**

without a disqualifying refund, chargeback, cancellation, fraud determination, or similar event.

Once the 90-day period has been completed and all other requirements are satisfied, the annual customer may become a QAPC.

---

# 6. Trials and Free Accounts

Free trials do not count as QAPCs.

Free accounts do not count as QAPCs.

A customer beginning through a trial may eventually become a QAPC after converting to a paid subscription and completing the applicable Qualification Period.

The qualification clock begins from the applicable paid subscription relationship, not merely from the date the person first created a free account or trial.

---

# 7. Canceled Customers

A canceled customer who is no longer an active paying customer on the Measurement Date does not count as a QAPC.

### Example

A Growth Partner historically acquired 110 qualifying customers.

Before milestone verification, 15 cancel.

On the Measurement Date:

**95 remain qualifying and active.**

GPP count:

**95 QAPCs**

The Growth I customer requirement has therefore not yet been satisfied.

---

# 8. Scheduled Cancellation

If a customer has requested cancellation but remains paid and entitled to service through the end of the current billing period, Voxe should ordinarily treat the customer as active until the paid subscription actually expires.

However, if the cancellation becomes effective before milestone verification is completed, the customer is no longer a QAPC for that measurement.

This keeps the calculation tied to the customer's actual subscription state rather than merely the existence of a cancellation request.

---

# 9. Failed Payments and Delinquency

A customer with a temporary payment-processing issue does not necessarily need to be permanently disqualified.

However, a customer whose subscription is materially delinquent, suspended for nonpayment, or no longer considered an active paid subscription should not count as a QAPC on the Measurement Date.

If the payment issue is resolved and the subscription returns to good standing, the customer may again qualify subject to the applicable rules.

---

# 10. Refunds

A fully refunded subscription payment generally does not count as successful paid subscription activity for the Qualification Period.

If a customer receives a full refund and terminates the subscription, the customer does not count as a QAPC.

Partial credits or legitimate service adjustments do not necessarily disqualify the customer, but Attributable ARR should reflect the actual recurring subscription arrangement applicable on the Measurement Date.

---

# 11. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

A customer associated with an unresolved chargeback or material payment dispute may be temporarily excluded from QAPC and Attributable ARR calculations while the dispute remains unresolved.

If the chargeback is resolved in Voxe's favor and the customer remains active and otherwise qualifying, Voxe may restore the customer to qualifying status.

Fraudulent chargebacks, coordinated payment manipulation, or similar conduct connected to the Growth Partner may result in further review or program termination.

---

# 12. Upgrades

If a QAPC upgrades its Voxe subscription, the customer remains:

**1 QAPC**

but its Attributable ARR increases to reflect the customer's current qualifying recurring subscription amount.

### Example

A qualifying customer originally pays:

**$49.99/month**

Attributable ARR:

**$599.88**

The customer later upgrades to:

**$119.99/month**

At the next applicable Measurement Date, assuming the upgraded subscription is active:

Attributable ARR becomes:

**$1,439.88**

The customer count remains:

**1 QAPC**

The customer does not need to restart the original three-payment qualification period solely because of an ordinary plan upgrade.

---

# 13. Downgrades

If a QAPC downgrades its subscription but remains an active paying customer, the customer can remain:

**1 QAPC**

but Attributable ARR is reduced to the current recurring subscription amount.

The customer ordinarily does not need to repeat the Qualification Period solely because of a normal plan downgrade.

---

# 14. Plan Changes

Ordinary movement between Voxe paid subscription plans does not, by itself, reset QAPC qualification.

The customer's Attributable ARR is calculated using the qualifying recurring subscription value applicable on the Measurement Date.

This allows ARR to reflect the actual current economic value of the customer base.

---

# 15. Reactivated Customers

A previously canceled customer who later returns to Voxe may again become eligible as a QAPC.

However, I recommend the following conservative rule:

> A customer who fully canceled and subsequently reactivates must complete a new Qualification Period before returning to QAPC status.

This prevents short cancellation/reactivation cycles from being used to manipulate milestone snapshots.

The customer still represents **one customer**, not a newly acquired additional customer.

---

# 16. Existing Voxe Customers

A Growth Partner generally cannot claim attribution for a customer who was already an active Voxe customer before the Growth Partner's involvement.

However, Voxe may expressly approve attribution where the Growth Partner can demonstrate that they created a materially new commercial relationship—for example, a separate organization or qualifying commercial expansion that Voxe elects to recognize.

Routine interaction with an existing customer does not transfer GPP attribution.

---

# 17. Customers Acquired Before GPP Admission

Customers acquired before a person's GPP Effective Date do not automatically count.

When admitting an existing affiliate or other contributor into GPP, Voxe may expressly establish an initial recognized customer portfolio.

For example:

An affiliate has already acquired 42 customers.

At GPP admission, Voxe verifies that:

35 are QAPCs.

Voxe may state in the Growth Partner's admission documentation:

> **Initial Recognized QAPCs: 35**

Those customers may then count toward future GPP milestones.

This decision should be made **at admission**, not retroactively after a partner approaches a milestone.

---

# 18. Customer Attribution

The Growth Partner claiming a customer must have made a meaningful contribution to that customer's acquisition.

Evidence may include:

- referral tracking;
- affiliate tracking;
- documented introductions;
- CRM records;
- campaign attribution;
- referral codes;
- sales records;
- customer confirmation;
- correspondence;
- billing/customer records; or
- other evidence accepted by Voxe.

Attribution should reflect actual contribution rather than merely being the last person to interact with a prospect.

---

# 19. Attribution Window

I recommend **not introducing a rigid universal cookie/window rule into GPP**.

Different acquisition methods work differently.

A direct introduction may be clearly attributable months later, while digital affiliate tracking may use a much shorter technical attribution window.

Therefore:

> The applicable Approved Attribution System and available evidence determine attribution, subject to Voxe verification.

The separate Affiliate Program may maintain its own cookie or referral window without automatically changing GPP attribution rules.

---

# 20. Multiple Growth Partners Claiming One Customer

A customer ordinarily counts toward only one Growth Partner's GPP milestones.

Where multiple Growth Partners claim the same customer, Voxe reviews the evidence.

Factors may include:

- who originated the relationship;
- who made the documented introduction;
- applicable referral records;
- campaign attribution;
- material contribution to conversion;
- timing;
- customer confirmation; and
- other relevant records.

For version 1 of GPP, **fractional QAPC splitting is not used by default**.

Voxe determines the primary Growth Partner attribution owner.

This keeps the milestone system understandable and administratively manageable.

---

# 21. Affiliate Attribution vs. GPP Attribution

Affiliate tracking may provide evidence for GPP attribution, but the two programs remain independent.

A customer's eligibility for affiliate commission does not automatically mean the customer qualifies as a GPP QAPC.

For example, an Affiliate Program might pay a commission beginning with the customer's first payment.

GPP may still require three successful monthly payments before the customer becomes a QAPC.

Similarly, changing affiliate providers does not erase valid GPP attribution records maintained by Voxe.

---

# 22. Affiliate Commission Does Not Reduce ARR

Where a Growth Partner separately receives an affiliate commission, that commission does not reduce Attributable ARR.

Example:

Qualifying subscription revenue:

**$100/month**

Annualized:

**$1,200 Attributable ARR**

If the Affiliate Program separately pays the Growth Partner a 30% commission, GPP still records:

**$1,200 Attributable ARR**

rather than $840.

Affiliate compensation is treated separately from GPP revenue attribution.

---

# 23. Discounts and Promotional Pricing

Attributable ARR should reflect the customer's **actual recurring subscription obligation**, not the undiscounted list price.

Example:

Normal subscription:

**$120/month**

Customer has an ongoing qualifying price of:

**$90/month**

Attributable ARR:

**$1,080**

not $1,440.

For a temporary promotion, Voxe may use the recurring amount applicable on the Measurement Date and the known recurring subscription terms when determining ARR.

The objective is to avoid overstating recurring revenue.

---

# 24. One-Time Revenue

One-time revenue does not ordinarily count toward Attributable ARR.

Examples include:

- setup fees;
- implementation fees;
- consulting;
- one-time customization;
- one-time training;
- non-recurring service charges; and
- other non-subscription purchases.

Those amounts may be valuable to Voxe, but GPP's standard milestone structure measures **recurring subscription revenue**.

---

# 25. Taxes

Sales tax, VAT, and similar taxes collected from customers do not count toward Attributable ARR.

Attributable ARR measures Voxe recurring subscription revenue rather than amounts collected on behalf of taxing authorities.

---

# 26. Credits

Credits that temporarily reduce an invoice should be evaluated according to their effect on the underlying recurring subscription.

A one-time goodwill credit does not necessarily permanently reduce the customer's ARR.

A permanent contractual reduction in recurring price does.

Voxe may review the underlying subscription terms when determining Attributable ARR.

---

# 27. Currency

If Voxe accepts subscription payments in multiple currencies, the applicable revenue may be converted into the reporting currency used for GPP milestone calculations.

The conversion methodology should be applied consistently.

For the initial program, I recommend **USD as the GPP reporting currency**.

---

# 28. Fraudulent or Artificial Customers

Customers created primarily to manipulate GPP milestones do not qualify.

Examples include:

- fabricated businesses;
- Growth Partner-controlled accounts without legitimate commercial purpose;
- multiple artificial accounts for the same underlying customer;
- circular payments;
- reimbursing supposed customers solely so they can purchase Voxe;
- coordinated subscriptions intended primarily to cross a milestone;
- fraudulent payment methods; or
- similar manipulation.

Voxe may investigate unusual acquisition patterns before verifying a milestone.

---

# 29. Self-Referrals

A Growth Partner purchasing Voxe for their own legitimate independent business should **not automatically count** toward a GPP milestone.

My recommendation is:

> Growth Partner self-referrals require explicit Voxe approval before being counted as QAPCs.

That prevents someone from creating businesses/accounts under their control to manufacture customer count.

---

# 30. Related Businesses

Multiple customers owned or controlled by substantially the same person or organization may be reviewed to determine whether they represent genuinely separate commercial customers.

Voxe may aggregate related accounts for QAPC purposes where necessary to prevent artificial customer-count inflation.

This does not mean legitimate franchises, portfolio companies, agencies, subsidiaries, or separate operating businesses can never qualify independently. It means Voxe can examine the commercial reality rather than relying only on account count.

---

# 31. Milestone Measurement

When a Growth Partner believes a milestone has been reached, the applicable customer portfolio is measured.

For every potentially qualifying customer, Voxe may determine:

**Attribution → Paid Status → Qualification Period → Active Status → Financial Standing → Eligibility → Current Recurring Revenue**

Only customers passing the applicable checks enter the final QAPC population.

Attributable ARR is then calculated from that qualifying population.

---

# 32. Both Gates Must Pass Simultaneously

The QAPC requirement and Attributable ARR requirement should be satisfied using the **same qualifying customer portfolio at the applicable Measurement Date**.

This prevents situations where a partner uses one historical group to satisfy customer count and a different historical period to satisfy ARR.

For Growth I:

> **≥100 current QAPCs AND ≥$100,000 current Attributable ARR**

must coexist at measurement.

The same principle applies to subsequent milestones.

---

# 33. Milestone Verification Record

When Voxe formally verifies a milestone, I recommend recording at minimum:

- Growth Partner;
- milestone;
- Measurement Date;
- verified QAPC count;
- verified Attributable ARR;
- verification date;
- verifier/approval;
- applicable cumulative equity eligibility;
- incremental award eligibility;
- definitive agreement status; and
- any material exceptions or special attribution decisions.

This creates an auditable record rather than relying on an affiliate dashboard screenshot.

---

# 34. Customer Churn After Formal Verification

Once a milestone has been formally verified and the resulting award has been documented under a definitive agreement, ordinary subsequent customer churn does not automatically reverse the milestone.

For example:

Growth I verified:

**108 QAPCs**  
**$112,000 ARR**

Three months later:

**96 QAPCs**  
**$101,000 ARR**

The previously verified Growth I milestone is not automatically erased.

However, the partner would need to satisfy the then-applicable Growth II requirements before Growth II could subsequently be verified.

---

# 35. Falling Below a Threshold Before Verification

The opposite rule applies before formal verification.

Example:

Tracking temporarily shows:

**102 QAPCs**

Before Voxe completes verification, three customers cancel.

Verified population:

**99 QAPCs**

Growth I has not been reached.

The milestone is based on the verified qualifying population, not the highest temporary dashboard number.

---

# 36. Subsequent Milestones

Previously verified milestones do not need to be earned again.

However, each subsequent milestone requires a new measurement of the Growth Partner's then-current qualifying customer portfolio.

Example:

Growth I was previously verified.

To reach Growth II, the Growth Partner must later establish:

**≥250 QAPCs AND ≥$250,000 Attributable ARR**

at the applicable Growth II Measurement Date.

---

# 37. Corrections and Data Errors

Voxe may correct genuine clerical, billing, attribution, or data-processing errors discovered during milestone verification.

Where a material error is discovered after verification, treatment should depend on the circumstances and applicable definitive agreement.

Ordinary good-faith data corrections should be distinguished from fraud or intentional misrepresentation.

---

# 38. Exceptions

There may eventually be unusual commercial arrangements that do not fit standard subscription/customer rules—for example, a large enterprise agreement covering many separate organizations.

Voxe may approve a written GPP treatment for such an arrangement.

Any material exception should be documented rather than silently changing the general program rules.

---

# 39. Verification Authority

Voxe is responsible for administering and verifying GPP milestones using reasonable available business records.

This authority is necessary because no affiliate provider, payment processor, CRM, or other individual system contains every fact necessary to determine GPP eligibility.

Voxe should apply these rules consistently and in good faith.

---

# 40. Governing Principle

Where a technical edge case is not expressly addressed by these rules, interpretation should remain consistent with GPP's underlying purpose:

> **Reward genuine, attributable, recurring, and durable business growth—not temporary activity or manipulation of program metrics.**
