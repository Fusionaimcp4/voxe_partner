# Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program
## Program Framework & Terms

**Draft — Subject to Definitive Agreement**

**Program:** Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program (“GPP”)  
**Company:** Blockchain Technology International LLC, operating as Voxe (“Voxe” or the “Company”)  
**Version:** Draft 1.0 — October 2026

---

## 1. Purpose of the Program

The Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program is a selective performance-based program intended for individuals who can make a meaningful and measurable contribution to the growth of Voxe.

The GPP is built around a simple principle:

> **Growth Partners who create measurable, attributable, and durable growth in Voxe may become eligible to earn ownership in the Company.**

The program does not award equity based merely on time spent, marketing activity, leads generated, introductions made, promises of future contribution, social-media activity, or participation in the program.

Instead, equity eligibility is based primarily on two measurable business outcomes:

1. **Qualifying Active Paying Customers (“QAPCs”) attributable to the Growth Partner; and**
2. **Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”) generated by those customers.**

Both requirements must be satisfied for a Growth Partner to reach an applicable milestone.

---

# 2. Selective Participation

Participation in the GPP is by invitation or acceptance by Voxe.

Submission of an Expression of Interest, participation in the Voxe Affiliate Program, referral of customers, discussions with Voxe, or receipt of GPP materials does not automatically make an individual a Growth Partner.

Voxe may admit individuals to the program through either of two principal paths.

### 2.1 Direct Invitation

Voxe may directly invite an individual to participate in GPP based on factors such as the individual's experience, relationships, distribution capabilities, market knowledge, business-development ability, sales or marketing capabilities, industry access, or other potential contributions to Voxe's growth.

A directly invited Growth Partner does not need to first participate in the Affiliate Program.

### 2.2 Affiliate-to-Growth-Partner Invitation

An individual may initially participate in the Voxe Affiliate Program and subsequently be invited into GPP after demonstrating an ability to acquire and retain customers or otherwise create meaningful value for Voxe.

Unless otherwise agreed by Voxe in writing when the individual enters GPP, customers acquired before the effective date of GPP participation do not automatically count toward GPP milestones.

Voxe may elect to recognize qualifying existing customers attributable to the individual at the time of admission to GPP.

---

# 3. Separate Voxe Programs

GPP, the Voxe Affiliate Program, and any Voxe investment opportunity are separate programs with separate purposes, terms, and rights.

## 3.1 Growth Partner Program

**Contribution:** measurable and attributable business growth.

**Potential benefit:** eligibility for performance-based equity awards.

Participation in GPP does not itself grant ownership in Voxe.

## 3.2 Affiliate Program

**Contribution:** attributable customer referrals or acquisitions.

**Benefit:** cash or other commissions according to the applicable Affiliate Program terms.

Affiliate compensation does not constitute equity.

Participation in the Affiliate Program does not automatically create GPP eligibility.

A Growth Partner may separately participate in the Affiliate Program. Where a person participates in both programs, affiliate compensation and GPP milestone calculations remain separate.

The commission rate, payout rules, tracking provider, qualification requirements, and other Affiliate Program terms are governed exclusively by the then-applicable Affiliate Program terms and may change independently of GPP.

## 3.3 Investment / Early Access SAFE

**Contribution:** investment capital.

**Potential benefit:** rights provided by the applicable investment agreement or SAFE.

A Growth Partner is not required to invest in Voxe.

Investment in Voxe does not satisfy GPP milestones or automatically make an investor a Growth Partner.

An individual may potentially participate in more than one Voxe program, but rights under one program do not automatically create rights under another.

# 4. Qualifying Active Paying Customer

For purposes of GPP, customer milestones are based on **Qualifying Active Paying Customers (“QAPCs”)**, not total historical signups or lifetime customer acquisitions.

A customer generally qualifies as a QAPC only when all applicable requirements below have been satisfied.

The customer must:

1. have been legitimately acquired and properly attributable to the Growth Partner;
2. have become a paying Voxe customer;
3. have completed the applicable qualification period;
4. remain an active paying customer on the applicable milestone measurement date;
5. remain in good financial standing;
6. not be subject to an unresolved refund, chargeback, payment dispute, or material delinquency;
7. not be fraudulent, fictitious, duplicated, artificially divided, internal, or a test account; and
8. otherwise satisfy the applicable GPP requirements in effect for that Growth Partner.

Free users, free trials, leads, prospects, mailing-list subscribers, demo requests, website visitors, social-media followers, impressions, unpaid accounts, inactive accounts, canceled customers, fraudulent accounts, duplicate accounts, and test accounts do not independently constitute QAPCs.

---

# 5. Customer Qualification Period

The purpose of the qualification period is to distinguish durable customer acquisition from temporary or low-quality signups.

### 5.1 Monthly Subscriptions

A customer on a monthly subscription generally becomes eligible to qualify as a QAPC after **three successful consecutive monthly subscription payments**, provided all other GPP requirements are satisfied.

### 5.2 Annual Subscriptions

A customer on an annual subscription generally becomes eligible to qualify as a QAPC after remaining an active paid customer for at least **90 days**, provided that the subscription has not been refunded, charged back, determined fraudulent, or otherwise disqualified.

The qualification period does not guarantee that a customer will qualify. All other eligibility and attribution requirements continue to apply.

---

# 6. Active-Customer Measurement

GPP milestones are based on the number of QAPCs who remain qualifying and active **at the time a milestone is measured and verified**.

They are not based solely on historical customer acquisition.

For example, if a Growth Partner historically acquired 135 customers but, at the applicable measurement date:

- 18 have canceled;
- 7 have not completed the qualification period;
- 5 are delinquent; and
- 105 remain QAPCs,

the Growth Partner's milestone customer count is **105 QAPCs**, not 135.

This approach is intended to reward durable customer and revenue growth rather than temporary acquisition volume.

---

# 7. Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue

In addition to the applicable QAPC requirement, each GPP milestone requires a minimum amount of **Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”).**

Attributable ARR generally means the current recurring subscription revenue associated with QAPCs attributed to the Growth Partner, annualized over a twelve-month period.

For a qualifying monthly subscription:

> **Current recurring monthly subscription amount × 12**

For a qualifying annual subscription:

> **Current recurring annual subscription amount**

Attributable ARR is measured using recurring subscription revenue rather than total cash collected.

Unless otherwise specified by Voxe, Attributable ARR excludes:

- taxes;
- refunds and credits;
- chargebacks;
- one-time implementation charges;
- one-time professional-service charges;
- non-recurring fees;
- promotional or complimentary value for which Voxe receives no recurring subscription revenue; and
- other amounts that do not represent recurring subscription revenue.

Affiliate commissions or other acquisition expenses are **not deducted** from Attributable ARR solely because the Growth Partner receives separate affiliate compensation.

---

# 8. Customer and Revenue Attribution

A customer must be reasonably attributable to the Growth Partner before that customer or the customer's recurring revenue can be included in a GPP milestone.

Attribution may be established through an **Approved Attribution System**.

For purposes of GPP:

> **“Approved Attribution System” means a referral, affiliate, CRM, billing, campaign, customer-record, documented-introduction, or other tracking or attribution system designated or accepted by Voxe from time to time.**

This definition allows Voxe to change technology providers without requiring modification of the fundamental GPP structure.

Evidence of attribution may include referral links, referral codes, CRM records, campaign records, documented introductions, customer records, billing information, or other reliable evidence accepted by Voxe.

A third-party affiliate platform is a measurement mechanism and does not independently determine equity rights.

### 8.1 Conflicting Attribution

Where multiple individuals claim attribution for the same customer, Voxe may review the available evidence and determine the appropriate GPP attribution.

Unless Voxe expressly approves another arrangement, a QAPC will ordinarily have only **one Growth Partner attribution owner** for GPP milestone purposes.

### 8.2 Manipulation

Artificial account splitting, self-referrals designed primarily to manipulate GPP metrics, fabricated customers, coordinated duplicate accounts, attribution manipulation, or similar conduct may result in exclusion of the affected customers and may constitute grounds for termination from GPP.

# 9. Growth Partner Milestones

The initial GPP milestone framework is:

| Milestone | QAPCs at Verification | Minimum Attributable ARR | Cumulative Equity Eligibility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Growth I** | 100 | $100,000 | **Up to 0.50%** |
| **Growth II** | 250 | $250,000 | **Up to 1.00%** |
| **Growth III** | 500 | $500,000 | **Up to 2.00%** |
| **Growth IV** | 1,000 | $1,000,000 | **Up to 3.00%** |

A milestone is not satisfied unless **both** the applicable QAPC requirement and Attributable ARR requirement have been satisfied and verified.

For example:

**100 QAPCs + $82,000 ARR**  
does not satisfy Growth I.

**82 QAPCs + $120,000 ARR**  
does not satisfy Growth I.

**100 QAPCs + $100,000 ARR**  
may satisfy Growth I, subject to verification.

---

# 10. Cumulative Equity Eligibility

GPP milestone percentages are **cumulative, not additive**.

Accordingly:

- Growth I represents cumulative eligibility of up to **0.50%**;
- Growth II represents cumulative eligibility of up to **1.00%**;
- Growth III represents cumulative eligibility of up to **2.00%**; and
- Growth IV represents cumulative eligibility of up to **3.00%**.

A Growth Partner who progresses through all four standard milestones does not accumulate 6.50%.

The standard cumulative GPP ceiling is **up to 3.00%**.

For purposes of incremental awards, the current framework therefore operates approximately as follows:

| Achievement | New Incremental Eligibility | Cumulative Eligibility |
|---|---:|---:|
| Growth I | Up to 0.50% | Up to 0.50% |
| Growth II | Up to additional 0.50% | Up to 1.00% |
| Growth III | Up to additional 1.00% | Up to 2.00% |
| Growth IV | Up to additional 1.00% | Up to 3.00% |

---

# 11. Milestone Verification

Milestones are subject to verification by Voxe.

When the applicable tracking information indicates that a Growth Partner may have reached a milestone, the appropriate status is:

> **Milestone Reached — Pending Verification**

Voxe may verify information including:

- customer attribution;
- subscription status;
- successful payments;
- qualification periods;
- recurring revenue;
- refunds;
- credits;
- chargebacks;
- cancellations;
- delinquency;
- duplicate accounts;
- fraudulent activity;
- account splitting;
- disputed attribution; and
- other information reasonably necessary to determine milestone eligibility.

A dashboard, affiliate report, automated calculation, email, conversation, website display, or other preliminary indication does not by itself constitute formal milestone verification or issuance of equity.

---

# 12. Equity Eligibility and Definitive Agreement

Formal verification of a GPP milestone establishes eligibility for the applicable equity award under the program, subject to execution of a definitive agreement approved by Voxe.

GPP participation itself does not issue equity, membership interests, stock, options, profits interests, or any other security.

The exact legal form of an equity award will be established in the applicable definitive agreement.

Until that agreement has been properly executed and any other applicable conditions have been satisfied, no GPP milestone, website display, program document, referral report, or communication should be interpreted as an issuance or transfer of ownership.

---

# 13. Vesting of Earned Milestone Awards

Each incremental equity award associated with a newly verified milestone will generally vest over **12 months**, beginning on the effective date specified in the applicable definitive agreement.

Each incremental milestone award may have its own 12-month vesting schedule.

For example:

A Growth Partner reaches Growth I and receives an award corresponding to **0.50% cumulative equity eligibility**.

That award begins its applicable 12-month vesting period.

The Growth Partner later reaches Growth II.

Because Growth II represents **1.00% cumulative eligibility**, the incremental Growth II award is up to an additional **0.50%**.

That incremental award may begin its own 12-month vesting period without restarting the vesting schedule of the Growth I award.

The same principle applies to subsequent milestone awards.

The exact vesting mechanics, vesting frequency, treatment upon termination, and other award-specific provisions will be contained in the applicable definitive agreement.

---

# 14. Good Standing During Vesting

Vesting is subject to the Growth Partner remaining in good standing under GPP and satisfying the requirements of the applicable definitive agreement.

For purposes of the program framework, good standing is not intended to require the Growth Partner to continuously maintain the exact customer count or ARR that originally qualified the Growth Partner for a verified milestone.

Instead, good standing generally means that the Growth Partner:

- has not voluntarily withdrawn from the applicable relationship where continued participation is required by the definitive agreement;
- has not been terminated for fraud, material misconduct, or material breach;
- has not materially manipulated customer or attribution information;
- has not materially violated applicable GPP requirements; and
- has not otherwise triggered a forfeiture provision contained in the definitive agreement.

The definitive agreement will control where its provisions differ from this general framework.

---

# 15. Customer Churn After Verification

Normal customer churn occurring after a milestone has been formally verified does not, by itself, reverse the verified milestone.

This distinction is intentional.

The active-customer and ARR requirements determine whether the Growth Partner **earned the milestone at the measurement date**.

The vesting provisions then govern the resulting equity award.

Voxe therefore does not intend to continuously increase and decrease an already verified milestone merely because ordinary customers subsequently upgrade, downgrade, or cancel.

However, later-discovered fraud, fabricated customers, manipulated attribution, concealed refunds, material misrepresentation, or similar misconduct may result in disqualification or other remedies as provided by the applicable definitive agreement.

---

# 16. Standard GPP Equity Ceiling

Under the standard GPP framework, the maximum cumulative equity eligibility for an individual Growth Partner is:

> **Up to 3.00%**

Growth or contribution beyond the Growth IV milestone may lead to discussion of a separate strategic, executive, employment, advisory, distribution, or other relationship.

Any such arrangement would be separately negotiated and documented.

Reaching Growth IV does not automatically create a right to receive equity beyond the standard 3.00% GPP ceiling.

# 17. Affiliate Compensation

A Growth Partner may separately participate in the Voxe Affiliate Program.

Where permitted, the same qualifying customer may generate:

1. affiliate compensation under the Affiliate Program; and
2. customer and recurring-revenue attribution toward GPP milestones.

These are separate forms of compensation.

Affiliate commissions are governed solely by the applicable Affiliate Program terms.

The GPP does not guarantee any particular affiliate commission percentage, payment duration, affiliate technology provider, payout schedule, or other affiliate term.

Voxe may replace or modify its affiliate provider or Affiliate Program independently of GPP.

Growth Partners may use lawful and appropriate methods to generate Voxe customers, subject to applicable GPP requirements and any brand or marketing guidance provided by Voxe.

Permitted activities may include, as appropriate:

- direct business development;
- professional relationships;
- customer referrals;
- partnerships;
- content;
- demonstrations;
- social media;
- communities;

- events;

- agency relationships; and

# 19. Prohibited Conduct

A Growth Partner may not use fraudulent, deceptive, unlawful, or materially misleading practices to generate customers or satisfy GPP milestones.

Prohibited conduct includes, without limitation:

- fabricated or fraudulent customers;
- unauthorized creation of customer accounts;
- artificial account splitting;
- manipulation of referral or attribution systems;
- false or misleading claims about Voxe;
- unauthorized guarantees;
- impersonation of Voxe or its personnel;
- unlawful spam or customer acquisition;
- misuse of customer information;
- falsification of revenue or payment information;
- undisclosed schemes intended primarily to manufacture milestone eligibility; or
- other material misconduct affecting Voxe, its customers, or the integrity of GPP.

Material violations may result in customer disqualification, milestone disqualification, suspension, termination, or remedies provided by the applicable definitive agreement.

---

# 21. Independent Relationship

Participation in GPP does not, by itself, create an employment relationship.

Unless separately agreed in writing, a Growth Partner is not entitled to:

- salary;
- wages;
- employee benefits;
- guaranteed working hours;
- expense reimbursement;
- an executive or employee title;
- guaranteed affiliate income; or
- guaranteed equity.

Nothing in GPP by itself authorizes a Growth Partner to bind Voxe or act as an officer, employee, legal agent, or authorized representative of the Company.

A Growth Partner may separately enter into an employment, contractor, advisory, executive, investment, affiliate, or other relationship with Voxe. Such relationship will be governed by its own agreement.

---

# 22. Program Modification

Voxe may modify, suspend, replace, or discontinue GPP prospectively as the Company, its products, pricing, business model, capitalization, or market conditions evolve.

Changes may include future milestone requirements, qualification rules, attribution methods, eligibility requirements, or availability of the program.

However, a prospective program change is not intended to retroactively eliminate a milestone award that has already been:

1. formally verified by Voxe; and
2. documented under an executed definitive agreement,

except as permitted by that agreement, including circumstances involving fraud, material misrepresentation, breach, attribution manipulation, or other specified misconduct.

---

# 23. No Guarantee of Future Value or Liquidity

Any equity award associated with GPP is subject to substantial risk.

Participation in GPP does not guarantee that Voxe will increase in value, raise additional capital, be acquired, conduct a public offering, make distributions, generate profits, or provide a market through which an ownership interest can be sold.

An equity interest may be illiquid and may ultimately have little or no economic value.

No statement in GPP should be interpreted as a guarantee of the present or future value of Voxe or of any equity award.

---

# 24. No Investment Required

Participation in GPP does not require the Growth Partner to purchase an investment in Voxe.

GPP equity eligibility is based on verified contribution under the program rather than an investment of capital.

Any opportunity to invest in Voxe, including any Early Access SAFE or other offering, is separate and subject to its own eligibility requirements, disclosures, documentation, and terms.

---

# 25. Program Records

Voxe may maintain records reasonably necessary to administer GPP, including customer attribution, recurring revenue, milestone calculations, verification results, award status, and related communications.

Third-party affiliate, billing, CRM, analytics, or other systems may be used as sources of information.

No single third-party system independently determines equity ownership.

In the event of a discrepancy, Voxe may review relevant underlying records before determining milestone eligibility.

---

# 26. Taxes and Individual Obligations

Equity awards, affiliate compensation, and other benefits may have tax consequences.

Each Growth Partner is responsible for obtaining their own tax, financial, and legal advice concerning participation in GPP and any resulting award.

Voxe does not provide personal tax, investment, or legal advice through the GPP materials.

Any required tax withholding, reporting, elections, or other obligations will be addressed as applicable to the definitive award structure.

# 27. Program Framework; Definitive Agreements Control

This document describes the intended business framework of the Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program.

It is **not itself an equity issuance, SAFE, investment agreement, employment agreement, or guarantee of compensation or ownership.**

Specific equity awards will be governed by applicable definitive agreements.

If a definitive agreement conflicts with this general program framework with respect to a particular award, the definitive agreement controls.

---

# 28. Core GPP Principle

The program is designed around a straightforward relationship between contribution and potential ownership:

> **Voxe Growth Partners earn the opportunity for ownership by creating measurable, attributable, and durable growth in the Company.**

The objective is to align Growth Partners and Voxe around long-term customer and recurring-revenue creation rather than short-term activity or temporary customer acquisition.
