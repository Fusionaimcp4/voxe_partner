# Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program
## Milestone & Equity Schedule

**Draft — Subject to Definitive Agreement**

**Program:** Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program (“GPP”)  
**Company:** Blockchain Technology International LLC, operating as Voxe  
**Version:** Draft 1.0 — October 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This schedule provides a concise reference for the standard performance milestones under the Voxe Equity-Eligible Growth Partner Program.

Growth Partners may become eligible for equity awards by creating measurable and durable growth in two areas:

**Qualifying Active Paying Customers (“QAPCs”)** and **Attributable Annual Recurring Revenue (“Attributable ARR”).**

Both requirements must be satisfied for a milestone to be achieved.

---

# 2. Standard Growth Milestones

| Milestone | QAPCs | Minimum Attributable ARR | Incremental Equity Eligibility | Cumulative Equity Eligibility |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Growth I** | 100 | $100,000 | Up to **0.50%** | Up to **0.50%** |
| **Growth II** | 250 | $250,000 | Up to **0.50%** | Up to **1.00%** |
| **Growth III** | 500 | $500,000 | Up to **1.00%** | Up to **2.00%** |
| **Growth IV** | 1,000 | $1,000,000 | Up to **1.00%** | Up to **3.00%** |

The standard maximum cumulative equity eligibility under GPP is **up to 3.00%**.

Performance beyond Growth IV may be considered separately by Voxe but does not automatically create additional GPP equity eligibility.

---

# 3. Both Requirements Must Be Met

Customer count and recurring revenue are simultaneous requirements.

For example, Growth I requires:

> **At least 100 QAPCs AND at least $100,000 Attributable ARR**

Therefore:

| Current Performance | Growth I |
|---|---|
| 100 QAPCs + $82,000 ARR | **Not reached** |
| 82 QAPCs + $120,000 ARR | **Not reached** |
| 100 QAPCs + $100,000 ARR | **Potentially reached — subject to verification** |
| 115 QAPCs + $127,000 ARR | **Potentially reached — subject to verification** |

Both measurements must be based on the same qualifying customer portfolio at the applicable Measurement Date.

---

# 4. What Counts as a QAPC

A customer generally becomes a QAPC only after satisfying the applicable qualification period and all other GPP eligibility requirements.

### Monthly customer

Requires:

> **Three successful consecutive monthly subscription payments**

### Annual customer

Requires:

> **At least 90 days as an active paid subscriber**

The customer must also remain active, paying, attributable to the Growth Partner, in good financial standing, and otherwise qualifying on the Measurement Date.

Free accounts, trials, leads, canceled customers, fraudulent accounts, duplicate accounts, and other non-qualifying accounts do not count.

---

# 5. How ARR Is Calculated

Attributable ARR is based on the **current qualifying recurring subscription value** of QAPCs at the Measurement Date.

For monthly subscriptions:

> **Current Monthly Recurring Subscription × 12**

For annual subscriptions:

> **Current Annual Recurring Subscription**

Therefore, normal plan changes affect ARR.

**Upgrade → ARR increases**

**Downgrade → ARR decreases**

The customer ordinarily remains one QAPC.

Affiliate commissions are calculated separately and are not deducted from Attributable ARR solely because the Growth Partner receives a commission.

---

# 6. Milestones Use Active Customers

GPP does not use lifetime customer-acquisition totals to determine milestones.

Example:

A Growth Partner historically acquired:

**127 customers**

At measurement:

- 12 canceled;
- 5 are delinquent;
- 4 have not completed qualification;
- 106 remain qualifying and active.

The GPP customer count is:

> **106 QAPCs**

not 127.

---

# 7. Milestone Verification

Reaching the numerical threshold does not automatically issue equity.

When program records indicate that a Growth Partner may have reached a milestone, the appropriate status is:

> **Milestone Reached — Pending Verification**

Voxe may then verify customer attribution, active subscription status, qualification periods, payments, recurring revenue, cancellations, refunds, chargebacks, duplicate accounts, fraud, and other applicable requirements.

Following successful verification, the milestone may be formally recorded.

---

# 8. From Milestone to Equity Award

The standard sequence is:

> **Performance → Milestone → Verification → Definitive Agreement → Equity Award → Vesting**

A dashboard, referral report, website calculation, email, or milestone counter does not itself issue equity.

A formally verified milestone establishes eligibility for the applicable award, subject to execution of the definitive agreement.

---

# 9. 12-Month Tranche Vesting

Each incremental milestone award generally vests over **12 months** from the effective date specified in its definitive agreement.

Under the working GPP structure, vesting will occur **monthly over 12 months with no additional cliff**, subject to the definitive agreement and the Growth Partner remaining in good standing.

### Growth I

Verified milestone:

**100+ QAPCs**  
**$100,000+ ARR**

Potential award:

> **Up to 0.50%**

The applicable 0.50% award begins its 12-month vesting schedule.

### Growth II

Later verified:

**250+ QAPCs**  
**$250,000+ ARR**

Cumulative eligibility:

> **Up to 1.00%**

Because up to 0.50% was associated with Growth I, Growth II creates an incremental award of:

> **Up to an additional 0.50%**

The new tranche begins its own 12-month vesting schedule. The Growth I vesting schedule does not restart.

### Growth III

Verified:

**500+ QAPCs**  
**$500,000+ ARR**

Incremental eligibility:

> **Up to an additional 1.00%**

Cumulative:

> **Up to 2.00%**

The new 1.00% tranche receives its own 12-month vesting schedule.

### Growth IV

Verified:

**1,000+ QAPCs**  
**$1,000,000+ ARR**

Incremental eligibility:

> **Up to an additional 1.00%**

Cumulative:

> **Up to 3.00%**

The new tranche receives its own 12-month vesting schedule.

---

# 10. Previously Verified Milestones

Normal customer churn after a milestone has been formally verified does not automatically reverse that milestone.

For example:

Growth I is formally verified at:

**108 QAPCs / $112,000 ARR**

Four months later the portfolio has:

**96 QAPCs / $101,000 ARR**

The previously verified Growth I milestone is not automatically canceled merely because of normal customer churn.

The applicable award continues to be governed by its definitive agreement and vesting terms.

However, the Growth Partner has **not reached Growth II** until a future measurement establishes at least:

**250 QAPCs AND $250,000 ARR.**

Fraud, material misrepresentation, manipulated attribution, fabricated customers, or similar misconduct may be treated differently under the definitive agreement.

---

# 11. Reactivated Customers

A customer who fully cancels and subsequently returns to Voxe must complete a **new Qualification Period** before returning to QAPC status.

Reactivation does not create a second customer.

The customer continues to represent:

> **1 QAPC**

once requalified.

---

# 12. Affiliate Compensation Is Separate

A Growth Partner may separately participate in the Voxe Affiliate Program.

Where permitted under both programs, a qualifying customer may simultaneously:

- generate affiliate compensation under the Affiliate Program; and
- contribute toward GPP QAPC and ARR milestones.

The programs remain legally and economically separate.

The Affiliate Program's commission rate, payout period, provider, attribution technology, and other terms are governed by the then-applicable Affiliate Program terms.

---

# 13. Standard GPP Ceiling

The standard Growth Partner milestone path ends at:

> **1,000 QAPCs + $1,000,000 Attributable ARR → Up to 3.00% cumulative equity eligibility**

Contribution beyond that level may lead to a separately negotiated strategic, executive, employment, advisory, distribution, or other relationship.

It does not automatically increase the standard GPP award beyond 3.00%.

---

# 14. Quick Reference

The entire standard GPP milestone system can therefore be summarized as:

> **100 + $100K → 0.50%**  
> **250 + $250K → 1.00%**  
> **500 + $500K → 2.00%**  
> **1,000 + $1M → 3.00%**

with four important rules:

**Customers must qualify.**  
**Customers must still be active.**  
**Customer count and ARR must exist together.**  
**Equity follows verification, definitive agreement, and vesting.**
